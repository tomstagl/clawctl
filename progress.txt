Notes: New newMCPCallHandler in cmd/clawctl/mcp_call.go wires the
previously-stubbed mcpserver.CallHandler seam to the same /v1/chat/completions

boundary in → traceparent header on the gateway call → envelope.traceparent
field → result _meta. Errors are in-band: a typed envelope.ToolError with
schema-enum Code, optional HTTPStatus, and clawctl ExitCode lands in

rather than getting an MCP transport reject. classifyMCPGatewayError +
mapHTTPStatusToErrorCode mirror the curl-aligned exit-code contract: 400
